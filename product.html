About the Trail Companion

Every walk, hike, or road trip comes with the same problem — your dog gets thirsty, and you're left cupping water in your hands or hunting for a bowl that's never there when you need it.

The Roven Trail Companion solves that with a simple 2-in-1 design: a leak-proof water bottle on one side, a detachable bowl on the other. Twist it open, and your dog has clean water in seconds — no spills, no mess, nothing extra to carry.

Whether it's the trail, the park, or a quick errand, Roven keeps hydration simple — for both of you.

Product Details

  • Capacity: 1000ml
  • Available colors: Grey, White, Yellow
  • Material: Food-grade BPA-free plastic
  • Clip included for leash/bag attachment
  • Detachable collapsible bowl
  • Twist-open mechanism — one-hand operable
Fill the bottle with fresh water before heading out. When your dog needs water, twist open the lid — the attached bowl pops out. Pour water into the bowl. When your dog is done, pour excess water back into the bottle, twist to close, and clip back onto your bag. That's it.
Rinse with warm water after each use. The bowl detaches fully for easy hand washing. Do not microwave. Not dishwasher safe at high heat — hand wash recommended to preserve the seal.
