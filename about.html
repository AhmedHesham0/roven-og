Our Story

Built for the dogs who don't sit still.

Roven is outdoor gear for dogs — made to keep up with every adventure, big or small.

Why We Started

An honest frustration turned into a brand.

Roven started with a simple frustration: every walk, hike, or road trip meant juggling a water bottle in one hand and a bowl in the other — assuming you remembered the bowl at all.

We believe outdoor gear for dogs shouldn't be an afterthought. It should be as ready for adventure as your dog is. That's why every product we make starts with one question: does this actually make life easier on the trail, in the car, or around the block?

Roven is for the everyday dog owner and the weekend hiker alike — because every dog deserves gear that keeps up.
